1. Agentic AI & Frameworks

LangGraph Surpasses CrewAI in Enterprise Adoption as the Production Standard for Stateful Agents

AliceLabs AI / PEC Collective / Towards AI — June–July 2026

LangGraph, the graph-based orchestration layer from the LangChain team, has overtaken CrewAI in GitHub stars during early 2026, driven by enterprise teams who need explicit branching, human-in-the-loop interrupts, and Postgres-backed checkpointing for production audit trails. Its directed-graph model — where agents are nodes and state flows along conditional edges — maps cleanly to approval workflows that cannot tolerate unpredictable execution paths. Teams frequently begin prototypes with CrewAI's more readable role-based syntax and later migrate to LangGraph when production-grade state management becomes the dominant constraint.

Microsoft is directing all new development to this framework while maintaining bug-fix and security support windows for existing AutoGen and Semantic Kernel deployments during migration.

Google ADK and the A2A Protocol Emerge as the Cross-Framework Interoperability Layer

GuruSup / Medium (Towards AI) — May 2026

Google's Agent Development Kit provides a hierarchical agent tree — a root agent delegating to sub-agents that can in turn spawn their own sub-agents — tightly integrated with Vertex AI and Gemini models. Its standout production feature is native support for the A2A (Agent-to-Agent) protocol, which enables an ADK agent to discover and invoke agents built in entirely different frameworks such as LangGraph or CrewAI through a standardised task interface. Industry analysis increasingly positions A2A as the emerging interoperability standard, with Google, Microsoft, Salesforce, Snowflake, and ServiceNow all announcing support for the protocol in mid-2026.

The Production Governance Gap: Why Agent Frameworks Alone Are Not Enough in 2026 CRITICAL

Cordum.io — Updated June 2026

A detailed analysis updated through June 2026 highlights the most costly oversight in enterprise agentic deployments: every major framework — LangChain, CrewAI, AutoGen, LlamaIndex, and Semantic Kernel — manages the agent reasoning loop competently, but none provides an out-of-process governance layer capable of approving or blocking dangerous tool calls before they execute. Before an autonomous agent can modify infrastructure, transmit customer data, approve expenditure, or trigger external transactions, organisations still require policy-before-dispatch mechanisms, explicit human approval states, and complete audit evidence trails. The practical recommendation is to pair any chosen agent framework with a separate Agent Control Plane that enforces these controls at runtime, not as post-processing guardrails.

The analysis also flags AutoGen conversation loops as the highest token-cost risk in production: without hard termination conditions, open-ended agent debates can consume five to ten times the expected token budget, a pattern that has caused significant budget overruns in real deployments. Teams currently evaluating framework TCO should build token-ceiling policies into their AutoGen configurations from day one, and factor governance tooling into the total platform cost before committing to a stack.

Research Google's TurboQuant Slashes KV Cache Memory Overhead — Could Reshape On-Device AI Economics

Crescendo AI News — ICLR 2026

Google's research team unveiled TurboQuant at ICLR 2026, an algorithm that significantly reduces the memory overhead caused by the KV cache — one of the most persistent bottlenecks in running large AI models with extended context windows. The algorithm uses a two-step process combining PolarQuant vector rotation and the Quantized Johnson-Lindenstrauss compression method, allowing models with massive context windows to run far more efficiently without requiring new hardware. Researchers believe the breakthrough could accelerate the broader industry shift from raw parameter scaling toward efficiency-first development, with compounding implications for on-device AI deployment and data centre operating costs.

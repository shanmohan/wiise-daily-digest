MCP Hits 5,800 Public Servers; Google's A2A Protocol Now Under Linux Foundation

Source: daily.dev  ·  Published March 2026

Complementing MCP, Google's Agent-to-Agent (A2A) protocol focuses on enabling agents to collaborate with each other, rather than with tools; the two protocols are now jointly governed under the Linux Foundation, forming a three-layer stack. A2A's "Agent Cards" — JSON manifests at a standardised well-known URL — allow cross-framework agent discovery without manual wiring, meaning a LangGraph research agent can work alongside a CrewAI writing agent without bespoke integration code.

Google ADK's A2A Native Support Enables True Cross-Framework Orchestration

Source: GuruSup / Cordum.io Framework Comparison  ·  Published April 2026

Google's Agent Development Kit, released in April 2025, provides a hierarchical agent tree where a root agent delegates tasks to sub-agents that can in turn spawn their own sub-agents — integrating tightly with Vertex AI, Gemini models, and Google Cloud services. Its headline differentiator is native A2A protocol support: an ADK agent can discover and invoke agents built in LangGraph or CrewAI through A2A's standardised task interface, enabling genuine cross-vendor orchestration without custom bridges. Production teams note that the abstraction currently trades fine-grained control for simplicity — there is no built-in checkpointing for long-running workflows and error handling is coarse-grained by comparison to LangGraph.

CTO Signal: Google ADK is the right choice if your stack is GCP-native and you need multimodal agent support; for hybrid-cloud or regulated workloads requiring audit trails, supplement with a control plane layer.

Source: Kiteworks / Wilson Sonsini / Clifford Chance  ·  Published January–April 2026

Enforcement of the EU AI Act is also scheduled to begin in 2026, ending the grace period that many multinational companies have relied upon.

Google ADK's A2A Protocol Enables Cross-Framework Agent Interoperability

GuruSup / Multiple Sources  ·  May–July 2026

Google's Agent Development Kit (ADK) has established a meaningful architectural differentiator through its native support of the Agent-to-Agent (A2A) protocol, which allows agents built in different frameworks to discover and communicate with each other through a standardised task interface. The ADK's hierarchical agent tree model — where a root agent delegates to sub-agents who may themselves spawn further sub-agents — maps well to enterprise workflow decomposition patterns and integrates tightly with Vertex AI and Google Cloud services.

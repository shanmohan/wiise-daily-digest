Google ADK's A2A Protocol Enables Cross-Framework Agent Interoperability

Source: Gurusup / Pydantic Analysis — May 2026

Google's Agent Development Kit (ADK) has distinguished itself in the framework landscape through native support for the Agent-to-Agent (A2A) protocol, allowing an ADK agent to discover and invoke agents built on entirely different frameworks — such as LangGraph or CrewAI — through a standardized task interface. This positions ADK as a natural fit for GCP-native teams who need interoperability with heterogeneous multi-vendor agent networks, and its hierarchical agent-tree model (where a root agent delegates to sub-agents that can have their own sub-agents) maps well to enterprise approval and oversight workflows. The trade-off is tighter GCP and Gemini lock-in compared with model-agnostic options, though the A2A protocol itself is open and framework-independent.

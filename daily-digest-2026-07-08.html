Source: GuruSup Multi-Agent Frameworks Guide — May 2026

Google's Agent Development Kit, released in April 2025, has matured into a production-grade option for GCP-native teams in 2026 — offering a hierarchical agent tree where a root agent delegates to sub-agents that may themselves have sub-agents, tightly integrated with Vertex AI and Gemini models.
